Why the seal area matters

Protecting weather stripping is about more than appearance.

Weather stripping helps limit air movement around a closed door. When pets repeatedly scratch that edge, the damage can affect both the look of the doorway and the condition of the seal.

Weather stripping

A recognized air-sealing fix

ENERGY STAR specifically lists weather stripping on doors as a simple way to address air leaks around the home.

Up to 10%

Whole-home efficiency context

ENERGY STAR says broader air-sealing and insulation improvements can provide up to 10% savings on annual energy bills.

Scratch Guard protects one vulnerable door-seal area and does not guarantee a specific energy savings amount. Sources: ENERGY STAR — Seal & Insulate and ENERGY STAR — Assess Opportunities.
