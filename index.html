Energy & comfort

Why protecting the seal area matters.

Seal damage is not only cosmetic. Air leaks around openings can affect comfort, efficiency, and the cost of keeping a home comfortable.

Up to 10%

Annual energy bills

ENERGY STAR says sealing air leaks and adding insulation can save up to 10% on annual energy bills.

15%

Heating & cooling

ENERGY STAR estimates average savings of about 15% on heating and cooling costs from broader air-sealing and insulation improvements.

10%–20%

Wasted energy costs

The U.S. Department of Energy says drafts, air leaks around openings, and outdated HVAC systems waste roughly 10% to 20% of annual home energy costs.

45%

Household energy spend

Heating-related costs make up nearly 45% of average household energy expenses, which makes the seal area worth protecting.
